# TalentHub AI Features

TalentHub AI is a comprehensive hiring platform that serves both candidates seeking their next opportunity and employers looking for top talent. Here is a detailed breakdown of the features.

## 🧑‍💻 Candidate Features

### 1. AI Career Copilot
- **Career Path Suggestions:** Get personalized career trajectory recommendations based on your current skills and experience.
- **Skill Gap Analysis:** Identify missing skills required for your desired roles and get actionable advice on how to acquire them.
- **Learning Resources:** Tailored course and material recommendations to help you upskill.

### 2. Smart Job Matching
- **Personalized Feed:** View job postings that closely align with your profile, ensuring you only see relevant opportunities.
- **Save for Later:** Bookmark interesting jobs to apply for them when you are ready.

### 3. Application Tracking
- **Real-time Activity Timeline:** Keep track of all your application statuses, saved jobs, and profile updates in one centralized dashboard.
- **Status Updates:** See when your application moves from 'Applied' to 'Under Review' or 'Interview'.

### 4. Profile Management
- **Modern Portfolio:** Build a standout profile with an intuitive, seamless interface.
- **Skills Showcase:** Highlight your technical and soft skills prominently to catch recruiters' eyes.

---

## 🏢 Employer Features

### 1. Advanced Applicant Tracking System (ATS)
- **Pipeline Management:** Move candidates seamlessly through customizable pipeline stages (Applied, Screening, Interview, Offer).
- **Candidate Directory:** A centralized view of all applicants across all active job postings.

### 2. AI-Enhanced Screening
- **Automated Fit Assessment:** Leverage AI to evaluate an applicant's fit based on their listed skills versus the job requirements.
- **Quick Filtering:** Instantly filter candidates based on experience, location, and department.

### 3. Dynamic Analytics Dashboard
- **Hiring Metrics:** Gain insights into total candidates, active jobs, and placement rates.
- **Pipeline Health:** Visualize how candidates are progressing through the hiring funnel.

### 4. Seamless Job Management
- **Job Posting:** Create, edit, and publish detailed job requirements with a rich text interface.
- **Vacancy Tracking:** Monitor the performance of your active job listings and see application velocity.
